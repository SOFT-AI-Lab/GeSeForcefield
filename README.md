# GeSeForcefield
Molecular Dynamics Forcefield for Ge–Se Chalcogenide Glasses

# Introduction
The development of reliable, yet computationally efficient empirical interatomic forcefields is key to facilitate the modeling of glasses. However, to date, no robust empirical potential is available for chalcogenide glasses. This arises from the fact that such glasses exhibit various difficulties, e.g., strongly directional covalent bonds, combination of homopolar and heteropolar bonds, varying coordination numbers, etc. Here, we adopt our recently proposed machine-learning-based optimization scheme to parameterize an empirical interatomic potential for Ge–Se chalcogenide glasses, wherein the forcefield parameters are optimized so as to ensure a good match with ab initio simulation data. We demonstrate that our new interatomic forcefield yields a level of accuracy that is comparable to that offered by ab initio simulations while being several million times more computationally efficient. This enables for the first time the simulation of large, slowly-quenched Ge–Se glasses, which reveals new insights in their atomic structure.
